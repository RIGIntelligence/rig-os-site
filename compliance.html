Colorado SB 24-205 Takes Effect February 2026

The first state-level AI governance law in the United States. If your business uses AI in hiring, lending, insurance, healthcare, or any "consequential decision" — you are required to inventory your AI systems, classify risk, conduct impact assessments, and maintain audit trails.

This is not future regulation. This is current law. The compliance deadline is now. Penalties include enforcement actions by the Colorado Attorney General and private right of action for individuals affected by non-compliant AI decisions.

What SB 24-205 Requires

The law applies to "deployers" of high-risk AI systems — any business that uses AI to make or substantially contribute to consequential decisions about consumers.

Requirement 01

AI System Inventory

Document every AI system in use, what decisions it influences, what data it processes, and who is responsible for its outputs. Most enterprises we work with have 10-40 AI tools across departments and cannot produce this inventory today.

Requirement 02

Risk Classification

Classify each AI system by risk level based on the consequentiality of the decisions it influences. High-risk systems (hiring, lending, insurance, healthcare) have the strictest requirements.

Requirement 03

Impact Assessments

Conduct and document impact assessments for high-risk AI systems. Evaluate potential algorithmic discrimination, accuracy, and the availability of human override.

Requirement 04

Consumer Notification

Notify consumers when AI is used in consequential decisions about them. Provide the ability to appeal and access human review of AI-assisted decisions.

Requirement 05

Audit Trails

Maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance. This means logging AI inputs, outputs, human oversight actions, and decision rationales for high-risk systems.

Requirement 06

Governance Program

Implement a governance program proportionate to the risk. This includes designated responsible individuals, training programs, and ongoing monitoring of AI system performance and fairness.
