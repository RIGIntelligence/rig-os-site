The Situation

A national fleet operator managing 20,000 vehicles was scheduling with spreadsheets and phone calls. Every schedule change required a dispatcher to manually check availability, compliance, and routes. Growth was stalling because the operation could not scale beyond what the dispatch team could handle manually.

What We Built

  • AI scheduling engine — Automated 82% of scheduling decisions using vehicle availability, driver hours, route constraints, and customer SLAs
  • Exception handling — Dispatchers handle only the 18% of schedules that require human judgment — late changes, special requirements, compliance edge cases
  • Real-time dashboard — Fleet-wide visibility into vehicle status, driver hours, route progress, and scheduling conflicts
  • Predictive demand — Volume forecasting that allows proactive scheduling instead of reactive dispatch

Results

  • 82% of scheduling decisions fully automated
  • 40% of dispatcher time recovered for higher-value work
  • Schedule change turnaround reduced from hours to minutes
  • On-time performance improved 12%
  • Able to scale fleet 30% without adding dispatch staff
