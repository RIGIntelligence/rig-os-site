Fleet IntOps: What We Deploy

Scheduling Automation

AI-powered scheduling that considers vehicle availability, driver hours, route constraints, customer SLAs, and real-time demand. Reduces manual scheduling by 60-82%.

Route Optimization

Dynamic route planning that adapts to traffic, weather, delivery windows, and vehicle capacity. Reduces fuel costs by 10-15% and improves on-time delivery rates.

Predictive Maintenance

Telematics-driven maintenance scheduling. Predict failures before they happen. Reduce roadside breakdowns by 30-40% and optimize maintenance spend.

Driver Performance

Safety scoring, fuel efficiency tracking, hours-of-service compliance monitoring, and coaching recommendations. Automated, not manual. Measured against fleet averages.

Dispatch Intelligence

Real-time dispatch recommendations based on current positions, load status, driver availability, and customer priority. Your dispatcher becomes a decision-maker instead of a data-entry clerk.

Operational Reporting

Automated daily fleet reports, cost-per-mile tracking, utilization dashboards, and compliance documentation. No more compiling spreadsheets at the end of the week.
