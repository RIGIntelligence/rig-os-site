Our Methodology

IntOps: Intelligence Operations

IntOps is the operating layer between AI tools and business accountability. Five layers. One system. Your team owns it when we leave.

Layer 01

Intelligence Foundation

Inventory every AI tool, model, and data source. Map who uses what, where decisions happen, and where risk concentrates. No audit trail, no deployment.

Layer 02

Workflow Engine

Automate the workflows that already have a business owner. One workflow in production is worth more than six AI demos in a slide deck.

Layer 03

Governance & Compliance

Risk classification, human-in-the-loop gates, and audit trails that satisfy Colorado SB 24-205 and enterprise security reviews. Compliance is not a legal memo — it is an operating system.

Layer 04

Performance Intelligence

Dashboards that show what is working, what is not, and what to do next. Every AI system gets measured by the business metric it was built to move.

Layer 05

Organizational Capability

Train the team that runs it after we leave. IntOps is not a retainer dependency — it is a capability transfer. You own the system, the playbook, and the institutional knowledge.
