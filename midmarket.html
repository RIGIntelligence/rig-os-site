The Path: Audit → Build → Embed

We do not start with a 6-month transformation plan. We start with your highest-ROI workflow, prove it works in production, and then decide together what to automate next.

Phase 01 — Process Automation Audit ($3,500 / 1 week)

Map Every Workflow

We interview your team, observe the workflows, and build a complete inventory with ROI estimates for each automation opportunity. You get a ranked list: highest ROI first, with effort estimates, risk flags, and a 90-day implementation sequence. This is not a generic AI assessment — it is specific to your business, your systems, and your people.

Phase 02 — One Workflow, Automated ($5K-$15K / 2-3 weeks)

Prove It Works

Pick the workflow at the top of the ROI ranking. We build it, integrate it with your existing systems (CRM, ERP, booking, project management — whatever you use), test it, deploy it, and measure it against the specific business metric it was built to move. If it does not work in production, you do not pay. That is the guarantee.

Phase 03 — Agent Build Pack ($15K-$40K / 4 weeks)

Scale What Works

Now that you have one win in production, we build the next 3-5 automations. CRM integrations, POS connections, ERP data flows, reporting dashboards, and team training. Each workflow gets an owner, a measurement framework, and a human escalation path.

Phase 04 — Fractional AI Operator ($8K-$15K/mo / 6-12 months)

Embed and Transfer

An embedded operator who runs your AI program — not as a consultant, but as a member of your team. Strategy, execution, measurement, and capability building. We design the roll-off from day one: full engagement → reduced scope → advisory only → handoff. The goal is making ourselves unnecessary.
