The Revenue Leaks Nobody Talks About

You are busy running a business. You do not notice the revenue walking past your door every day. Here is where most local businesses leak money without knowing it.

Missed Calls

8-12 calls per week go to voicemail during peak hours. At a $300 average job value, 35% of those callers would have booked. That is $4,000-$6,000/month walking away.

No-Shows

10-20% of appointments no-show without a reminder system. 40% of those are recoverable with an automated confirmation and rebooking sequence.

Silent Happy Customers

Your best customers leave satisfied and never tell anyone. No review request. No referral link. No follow-up. Every happy customer who does not leave a review is a referral you never got.

No Win-Back

Customers who visited 60+ days ago are gone unless you re-engage them. Most local businesses have zero automated win-back cadence. These are people who already liked you enough to come once.

Invisible Referrals

Word of mouth is your best channel but you cannot track it, measure it, or incentivize it. Without trackable referral links, you are guessing at your most profitable acquisition source.

No Reporting

You have no idea which source brings the most revenue, which service has the highest no-show rate, or which employee generates the most reviews. You are flying blind and making decisions on gut feel.

The Six-System Machine

Six interlocking systems. Installed in 30 days. Zero upfront cost. Each system addresses a specific revenue leak and compounds with the others.

System 01

Capture

Automated review requests after every visit. Timed to arrive when satisfaction peaks — not the next day, not the next week. Smart routing sends happy customers to Google, unhappy ones to a private feedback form so you can fix it before it goes public.

System 02

Fill

24/7 AI booking assistant. When someone calls after hours or during a rush, the system answers, books the appointment, and confirms. No missed calls. No voicemail graveyard. Integrates with your existing booking system — we do not make you switch.

System 03

Refer

Trackable referral links for every customer. When Sarah tells her friend about you and the friend books, you know Sarah sent them. You can thank Sarah, reward her, and measure exactly how much revenue word-of-mouth generates.

System 04

Retain

30/60/90-day win-back sequences. Customers who have not returned get a personalized sequence — not a generic blast, but targeted messages based on what they booked last, when they visited, and what makes sense to offer next.

System 05

Save

No-show reduction through smart reminders. Confirmation 48 hours before. Reminder 2 hours before. Easy reschedule link instead of cancel. For most businesses, this cuts no-shows by 40-60% in the first month.

System 06

Grow

Monthly performance report and quarterly business review. See exactly which systems are producing, which need tuning, and where the next opportunity is. This is not a dashboard you never check — it is a 30-minute review with your RIG advisor every month.
