The Healthcare AI Challenge

Healthcare has the most to gain from AI and the most to lose from getting it wrong. High stakes, heavy regulation, complex workflows, and a workforce that is already burned out. Generic AI consulting does not survive contact with clinical reality.

Patient Flow Bottlenecks

ED boarding, bed assignment delays, discharge process failures, and scheduling inefficiencies that create 2-4 hour wait times and LWBS rates above 10%.

Clinical Documentation Burden

Clinicians spend 2+ hours per shift on documentation. AI-assisted documentation must be accurate, auditable, and integrated with EHR workflows — not a standalone tool.

Staffing & Scheduling

Nurse scheduling is a 40-hour-per-month administrative task. Predictive demand models and automated scheduling can recover 80%+ of that time while improving coverage.

Compliance & Governance

AI in healthcare decisions requires human-in-the-loop oversight, audit trails, bias monitoring, and regulatory compliance. Colorado SB 24-205 adds state-level requirements on top of federal regulations.

Healthcare IntOps: What We Deploy

Every healthcare AI deployment gets governance first, workflows second, and measurement throughout. No exceptions.

Patient Flow Optimization

Real-time demand prediction, automated bed assignment, discharge workflow acceleration, and LWBS reduction. Measured by: wait time, LWBS rate, throughput, patient satisfaction.

Clinical Workflow Automation

Documentation assistance, order set optimization, referral automation, and care coordination workflows. Integrated with Epic, Cerner, or your EHR. Human review on every clinical output.

Scheduling Intelligence

Predictive demand-based nurse scheduling, OR block optimization, and clinic template management. Reduces scheduling admin by 80% while improving coverage quality.

Revenue Cycle Automation

Prior authorization automation, denial management, coding accuracy improvement, and charge capture optimization. Measured by: clean claim rate, days in AR, denial overturn rate.

Compliance Infrastructure

AI system inventory, risk classification, audit trail infrastructure, human-in-the-loop gate design, and SB 24-205 compliance documentation. Required before any clinical AI deployment.

Operational Reporting

Automated quality metrics, department dashboards, board reporting, and regulatory compliance documentation. Every AI system measured by the clinical or operational outcome it was built to move.
