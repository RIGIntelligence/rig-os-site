What the Law Requires

The Short Version

Colorado SB 24-205 requires businesses using AI in "consequential decisions" — hiring, lending, insurance, housing, healthcare, education — to identify high-risk systems, perform impact assessments, and implement governance. Non-compliance carries enforcement risk from the Colorado AG.

Who It Affects

Any business operating in Colorado that uses AI (including third-party tools) in decisions that materially affect consumers. This includes chatbots that triage support tickets, scoring systems for loan applications, automated hiring tools, and dynamic pricing algorithms.

What You Get

AI System Inventory

Every AI system in your organization — including third-party tools you might not realize use AI. Chatbots, recommendation engines, scoring systems, automated decisioning.

Risk Classification

Each system classified per SB 24-205: high-risk (consequential decisions), moderate-risk (consumer-facing), or low-risk. Clear categories, no ambiguity.

Impact Assessment Framework

For each high-risk system: what data it uses, what decisions it influences, what biases could emerge, and what documentation is required.

Compliance Gap Analysis

Where you are today vs. where the law requires you to be. Specific gaps with specific remediation steps.

Governance Template

A starter AI governance policy customized for your business. Roles, responsibilities, review cadences, and escalation procedures.

Remediation Roadmap

Priority-ordered: what to fix now (before enforcement), what to build in Q1, and what to implement by year-end. Realistic timelines, not panic-driven.
