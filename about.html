About RIG

We build the operating system.
Not the slide deck.

Rodgers Intelligence Group was founded on a simple conviction: AI fails not because the technology is wrong, but because there is no operating model to make it stick. We build that model.

The Origin

Mike Rodgers spent fifteen years inside healthcare operations — from Oracle Health to regional systems serving hundreds of thousands of patients. He watched organizations spend millions on AI tools that never made it past the pilot stage.

The pattern was always the same: an executive gets excited about AI, a vendor runs a proof of concept, the demo looks great, and then nothing happens. The tool sits in a Slack channel. The champion moves to a new role. Six months later, someone asks "whatever happened to that AI project?" and nobody has a good answer.

The problem was never the technology. The problem was the absence of what we now call IntOps — Intelligence Operations. The operating layer that makes AI a permanent business function instead of a temporary vendor experiment.

RIG exists to solve that problem at every altitude. For a $3M medspa, IntOps means a Six-System Machine that captures reviews, fills appointments, and drives referrals — zero upfront. For a $2B healthcare network, it means governance frameworks, compliance systems, and multi-department AI programs with audit trails and human-in-the-loop gates.

Same methodology. Same conviction. Different scale.

What We Actually Do

We are not a staffing firm. We are not a dev shop. We are not a strategy consultancy. We are an AI operations company that builds systems your team can run after we leave.

Diagnose

We inventory your AI exposure, map workflow opportunities, and build ROI-ranked implementation plans. Every engagement starts with measurement, not assumptions.

Build

We build AI workflows that work in production — not demos that work in a conference room. Integrated with your existing stack. Measured by the business metric they were built to move.

Transfer

We train your team, document the playbook, and design a structured roll-off. IntOps is a capability your organization owns — not a retainer you rent from us.
