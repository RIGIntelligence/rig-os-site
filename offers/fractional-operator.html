What a Fractional AI Operator Does

Strategy

Identify and prioritize AI opportunities across your business. Build the roadmap. Sequence the investments. Align with business goals, not technology trends.

Execution

Build and deploy automations. Manage vendor relationships. Run pilots. Scale what works. Kill what doesn't. Hands-on, not slide-deck consulting.

Measurement

Track ROI on every automation. Report to leadership monthly. Adjust strategy based on real data. Prove the investment is working — or pivot fast.

Team Development

Train your people to manage AI systems. Build internal playbooks. Transfer knowledge systematically. The goal is your independence, not our recurring revenue.

Vendor Management

Evaluate AI tools. Negotiate contracts. Manage implementations. You don't need to become an AI expert — you need someone who already is.

Governance

Build AI policies, review processes, and risk frameworks. Especially critical for regulated industries and Colorado SB 24-205 compliance.

The Roll-Off Model

Traditional consulting creates dependency. We engineer the opposite. Every engagement has a structured roll-off baked in from day one.

Phase 1

Full Engagement — 100% Rate

Months 1-4. Embedded in your team 3-4 days/week. Strategy, execution, building. This is the sprint.

Phase 2

Reduced Engagement — ~62% Rate

Months 5-8. Your team handles day-to-day. We focus on optimization, edge cases, and capability gaps. 2 days/week.

Phase 3

Advisory — ~33% Rate

Months 9-12. Monthly strategy reviews. Quarterly planning. On-call for complex decisions. Your team runs everything.

Phase 4

Handoff — Complete

Full documentation, playbooks, and trained team. Optional ongoing advisory retainer. But you don't need us anymore. That's the point.
